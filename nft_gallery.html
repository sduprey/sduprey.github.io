Painted animals

A generative series — elephants, monkeys and giraffes rendered in the hands of Basquiat, Haring, Miró, Banksy, Klee, Kandinsky, Pollock, Van Gogh and more. Held across four Solana wallets, pulled live from chain.

Every piece was synthesised running Stable Diffusion v1‑4 (CompVis/stable-diffusion-v1-4) through Hugging Face diffusers. The pipeline is loaded in half‑precision on a CUDA GPU and driven by a fixed torch.Generator seed (2048), so each run is reproducible. The strongest frames were then upscaled and minted on Solana.
